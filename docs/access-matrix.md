# Preview role access

These are fictional browser previews, not real sign-in or production authorisation. `src/access/policy.ts` is the single entitlement source for SharePoint gates, Teams adapters, Wiki areas, the Frontline Sales Portal, projects, departments and incident participation. `catalog.ts` resolves exactly five selectable roles; old author/contact IDs resolve to the ordinary default, never an inherited stored privilege. Other fictional staff remain in `peopleDirectory` for authorship and focal points.

**Default: General Staff Member — Taylor Brooks.** Role selection is external to the simulated products. Each role retains its own department and unit; switching does not join unrelated teams. Technical administration and executive seniority do not grant business entitlement.

Project file grants are explicit, including ordinary internal examples: Taylor may read PRJ-2026-021 and 031 plus one historical project; Maya participates in the customer-platform renewal; Robin has selected strategic/financial oversight, not all project files; Jamie has the new-starter project; Devon has technical customer-platform and search projects. Reading a project does not automatically join its conversation.

The Sales & Marketing denied state offers a contextual route to the Portal, not access to the denied destination. Frontline and workspace focal-point contacts precede urgent Service Desk escalation. The role signpost uses `allowedPreviewRoles(resource)` for the exact denied area and never appears for ordinary team membership or where none of the five roles is authorised. Independent representation deliberately has no preview-role entitlement.

There is no omniscient role. Credentials, individual records and real case data are not included in fixtures; authorised demonstrations use synthetic administration context and methods. No Infrastructure Wiki is introduced by the moderation workspace.
